Who Is Arogs

A Leader Born From This Soil

Arogs is not a stranger to the corridors of student governance at OAU. From the faculty level to the broadest cross-faculty platforms, this is a candidate who has shown up, delivered, and earned every endorsement through action, not words.

A product of Obafemi Awolowo University's finest academic tradition, Arogs combines intellectual rigour with grassroots empathy — understanding that the student body is not a monolith but a rich, diverse community deserving leadership that sees every voice.

The slogan Rise With IMPACT is not just a campaign phrase — it is a philosophy. IMPACT means: Integrity, Movement, Presence, Action, Community, Transformation.

"Every student at Great Ife deserves a union that works as hard as they do."

Looking Forward

The IMPACT Agenda

Six pillars. One mission. A union that works for every single student at Great Ife.

01
Welfare & Mental Health

Establish a dedicated Student Wellness Centre, expand free counselling sessions, and create an emergency hardship fund for students in acute need.

02
Academic Excellence

Lobby for a student-accessible grading transparency portal, structured departmental tutorials, and a formal student-faculty dialogue calendar every semester.

03
Infrastructure & Safety

Advocate for rehabilitated study spaces, functional hostel facilities, and a university-wide emergency response protocol co-managed by the union.

04
Enterprise & Opportunity

Launch the IMPACT Internship Board — a partnership with alumni and corporate entities to create paid internship pipelines for Great Ife students.

05
Inclusion & Representation

Ensure every faculty, minority group, and special-needs student has a direct channel into union decision-making — no voice left behind.

06
Transparency & Accountability

Publish monthly union activity reports, open the budget to student review, and hold quarterly town halls — leadership that has nothing to hide.

The Manifesto

Not promises — pledges backed by a track record that makes them believable.

MAN
IFES
TO
01
₦500K Emergency Student Fund

Establish a ring-fenced emergency welfare fund accessible to students in documented financial or medical crisis, disbursable within 48 hours of application.

02
Dedicated Careers & Internship Office

A physical and digital IMPACT Career Hub — staffed, resourced, and actively connecting students to post-university opportunities every single semester.

03
Open Budget Policy

All union financial allocations published monthly. Every naira accounted for. Students can submit queries and receive documented responses within 7 days.

04
A Town Hall Every Semester

Compulsory open town halls — not optional — where the union leadership faces unfiltered student questions and responds with concrete action plans.

05
Mental Health Integration

Partner with the university health centre to embed mental health services into student life — with reduced stigma campaigns and structured peer support training.

06
Faculty Rep Empowerment Programme

Invest in training and resourcing faculty representatives so they can actually deliver for their constituents — not just attend meetings.

07
IMPACT Digital Platform

A unified student portal for union services, petition submission, event registration, and feedback — so you never have to physically chase down a union official to be heard.
