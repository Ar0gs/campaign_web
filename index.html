Who Is Arogs

Born From
This Soil

Arogs is not a stranger to the corridors of student governance at OAU. From the faculty level to the broadest cross-faculty platforms, this is a candidate who has shown up, delivered, and earned every endorsement through action — not words.

A product of Obafemi Awolowo University's finest tradition, Arogs combines intellectual rigour with grassroots empathy — understanding that the student body is a rich, diverse community deserving leadership that sees every voice.

The slogan #RiseWithIMPACT is not just a campaign phrase — it is a philosophy built on Integrity, Movement, Presence, Action, Community, and Transformation.

"Every student at Great Ife deserves a union that works as hard as they do."

The Agenda

Six Pillars.
One Mission.

A union built to work for every single student at Great Ife — without exception.

01
Welfare & Mental Health

Establish a dedicated Student Wellness Centre, expand free counselling sessions, and create an emergency hardship fund for students in acute need.

02
Academic Excellence

Lobby for a grading transparency portal, structured departmental tutorials, and a formal student-faculty dialogue calendar every semester.

03
Infrastructure & Safety

Advocate for rehabilitated study spaces, functional hostel facilities, and a university-wide emergency response protocol co-managed by the union.

04
Enterprise & Opportunity

Launch the IMPACT Internship Board — a partnership with alumni and corporates to create paid internship pipelines for Great Ife students.

05
Inclusion & Representation

Ensure every faculty, minority group, and special-needs student has a direct channel into union decision-making — no voice left behind.

06
Transparency & Accountability

Monthly activity reports, open budgets, and quarterly town halls. Leadership with nothing to hide and everything to prove.

The
Manifesto

Not promises — pledges backed by a track record that makes them believable.

MAN
IFES
TO
01
₦500K Emergency Student Fund

A ring-fenced emergency welfare fund accessible to students in documented financial or medical crisis — disbursable within 48 hours of application.

02
Dedicated Careers & Internship Office

A physical and digital IMPACT Career Hub — staffed, resourced, and actively connecting students to post-university opportunities every semester.

03
Open Budget Policy

All union financial allocations published monthly. Every naira accounted for. Students can submit queries and receive documented responses within 7 days.

04
A Town Hall Every Semester

Compulsory open town halls — not optional — where the union leadership faces unfiltered student questions and responds with concrete action plans.

05
Mental Health Integration

Partner with the university health centre to embed mental health services into student life — with stigma-reduction campaigns and structured peer support training.

06
Faculty Rep Empowerment Programme

Invest in training and resourcing faculty representatives so they can actually deliver for their constituents — not just attend meetings.

07
IMPACT Digital Platform

A unified student portal for union services, petition submission, event registration, and feedback — so you never chase a union official to be heard.
